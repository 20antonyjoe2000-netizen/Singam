Meet your coach

A lion's patience.

Singam was built on a simple belief: endurance isn't about training harder than everyone else — it's about training smarter, longer, and with a plan that actually fits your life.

Over fourteen years, Coach Arjun "Singam" Raman has guided beginners across their first finish line and elite athletes to Boston and Kona qualification. Every plan is hand-built, reviewed weekly, and adapted to the data your body sends back.

The approach

Train smart. Recover hard. Show up every day.

Four principles shape every plan we write — whether it's your first 5K build or a sub-10 Ironman.

01

Built around your life

Plans flex to your schedule, not the other way around. Real progress on the hours you actually have.

02

Recovery is training

We program rest as deliberately as intervals. Adaptation happens when you stop, not when you grind.

03

Data-informed, human-led

We read the numbers, then talk like people. Heart rate and power guide it; judgement drives it.

04

Consistency compounds

No hero weeks, no burnout. Small, repeatable sessions stacked over months win the start line.
